sciClaw + Automacy — where the winnable demand actually is

Both sites' obvious keywords turned out to be the wrong targets. The real opportunities sit one seed over — and in both cases they're competitor-brand and comparison terms at difficulty 0–15, the same pattern that worked for MedicalFigs.

⚠ The two surprises

1. "AI research agent" is a dead end for sciClaw. Only 18 keywords total; the head term is just 170/mo. It's an emerging phrase with high CPC ($13–14) but almost no search volume. Do not build the site around it.
2. "Raycast hyper key" is a dead end for Automacy. raycast hyper key = 30/mo, hyper key mac = 30, most related terms 10–20. Splitting the guide into pages targeting these would earn almost nothing.
But both sites have a strong adjacent niche — found by testing the neighbouring seed. Details below. The guide split is still right for Automacy — just aimed at different keywords.
